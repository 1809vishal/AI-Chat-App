EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim reimbursement for reasonable business-related expenses including travel, client meals, accommodation, and approved software subscriptions.

2. Travel
Domestic travel must be booked in economy class. International flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy with manager pre-approval. All travel must be booked at least 7 days in advance where possible.

3. Meals
Meal expenses during business travel are reimbursed up to $50/day (domestic) and $75/day (international), with itemized receipts required for claims over $25.

4. Submission Deadline
All expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted after this window require VP-level approval.

5. Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol (except at approved client entertainment events), personal entertainment, and traffic/parking fines are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

6. Approval Chain
Expenses under $200 require direct manager approval. Expenses between $200 and $1000 require department head approval. Expenses above $1000 require VP and Finance approval.
