REMOTE WORK POLICY

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their 90-day probation period and hold roles not requiring physical on-site presence are eligible to apply for remote work arrangements.

2. Hybrid Schedule
The standard company arrangement is hybrid: employees are expected to work from the office a minimum of 3 days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday), with Monday and Friday flexible for remote work, subject to manager approval.

3. Fully Remote Requests
Fully remote arrangements (5 days/week) require director-level approval and are evaluated on a case-by-case basis considering role requirements and team needs.

4. Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time home-office setup allowance of $300 for employees approved for regular remote work. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and reliable internet connection.

5. Working Hours
Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time, regardless of flexible start/end times.

6. Security Requirements
All remote work must be conducted using company-issued devices with VPN enabled. Use of public Wi-Fi without VPN for accessing company systems is prohibited.
